## Result

- Fault classes caught on every scenario: **13/13**
- Injected faults caught: **650/650**
- Clean solver plans falsely blocked: **0/50** (clean plans with any failed check: 0/50; blocked by the critical-shortage safety gate: 0/50)

"Caught" means the expected check failed. Critical checks also block the plan. `plan_worse_than_optimum` and `prompt_injection_in_report` are deliberately non-blocking: a worse-than-optimal plan is shown to the operator as a high-severity finding, and instruction-like report text is flagged, never obeyed.

## Corpus

50 scenarios from `generate_scenario(seed)` for seeds 0–49, adjusted so every mutator applies: incident 0 is critical, every demanded unit type has an idle surplus unit, one offline unit exists per type, and each incident has a templated field report. The clean plan is the CP-SAT optimum with verbatim citations of retrieved knowledge and a SITREP rendered from the plan.

## Limits of this evidence

- Faults are injected one at a time by mutators written alongside the verifier. This shows each check does its job; it is not an adversarial evaluation of a live model.
- Scenarios use the synthetic 0–100 grid and Euclidean travel times.
- SITREP numbers are checked against a fixed keyword grammar; a draft that phrases a number differently fails as unattributed rather than being understood.
- Instruction detection is pattern-based and will miss paraphrased injections.
